# Primary Signal

**Cyber-security developments, with sources and evidence in view.**

**[Explore the fictional tour →](https://mravalanche.github.io/primarysignal/)**

A cyber-security publication built around the story behind each report.
Primary Signal brings related reporting together, keeps original sources close
to each account, and makes the evidence behind important claims easy to inspect.

We want readers to see what changed, why it matters, and where the information
came from without having to sort through duplicate coverage.

## The reading experience

- **Stories, with context.** Coverage of one development belongs together, with
  a clear account of what is known and what remains uncertain.
- **Sources in view.** Source trails point back to original reporting and
  distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- **A deliberate reading order.** The planned publication will put material
  developments first and make recent changes easy to find.

## In development

The public reading desk currently runs with fictional sample stories. It shows
the intended layout and source trails, but it is not a live news service yet.
Live feeds, editorial automation, and release controls are still being built
and tested. The [delivery plan](docs/PLAN.md) describes the work ahead.
